Which of the following is a beta-blocker?

Correct!
Score: 2/10

Explanation:

What is the mechanism of action of ACE inhibitors?

Hint: Think about angiotensin

Answer:

ACE inhibitors block the angiotensin-converting enzyme, preventing the conversion of angiotensin I to angiotensin II, leading to vasodilation and reduced blood pressure.

Mnemonic:

"ACE blocks the race" - stops the angiotensin conversion race
